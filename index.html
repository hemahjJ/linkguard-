    Quick safety checks

    Read the full domain

    Look closely at the domain before the final .com, .in, .org, or other ending.

    Use a trusted path

    Open important accounts from your saved bookmark or the official app, not from an unexpected message.

    Pause on urgency

    Unexpected deadlines, prize claims, and account-lock threats are common phishing tactics.
